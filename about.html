Our Story • Family-Owned & Operated in Lebanon, MO

Meet Matt & Pam Dawson

When Matt and Pam Dawson took over the shop at 1060 Springfield Rd (formerly known as 4 Wheels Auto and Tire Express) and launched Dawson's Hot Wheels, they set out with a simple promise to Lebanon and Laclede County drivers:

“Hello, Matt and Pam Dawson here. We are the new owners of Dawson's Hot Wheels (formally 4 Wheels Auto and Tire Express) located at 1060 Springfield Rd. here in Lebanon, MO. We are a true family owned small business that would love nothing more than to serve you with your tire and automotive needs! We will be offering services such as new and used tires, wheels, flat repairs, alignments, suspension lifts, and auto repair. Our mission is to provide our customers with quality service at the most affordable price without surprises or nonsense gimmicks.” — Matt & Pam Dawson, Official Business Statement

From helping local farmers and boaters find in-stock trailer tires to helping stranded I-44 travelers get back on the road after an unexpected radiator breakdown, Dawson's Hot Wheels has earned a 4.8-star reputation across 51 Google reviews—and proudly supports Lebanon High School student-athletes as a Jacket Nation Sports Gold Sponsor.
